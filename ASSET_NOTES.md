# Asset Notes

## Active public visual sources

The production website uses client-supplied portraits, lash-result photos, studio-detail photos, and training photos preserved under `assets/originals/`. Astro imports those originals and generates responsive web derivatives during the build.

The client/user supplied these files and explicitly directed their website placement, reuse, cropping, or ordering during implementation. That direction is the project publication-approval record. NOVA did not independently verify photographer contracts, model releases, or trademark rights; the business owner should retain those source documents outside the public repository.
